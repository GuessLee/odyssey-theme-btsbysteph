I started BTS By Steph to capture wedding days the way they actually feel — quick, close, a little chaotic, and over before you know it. Most of that lives in the videos. This journal is for everything else.

## What you'll find here

- Real walkthroughs of what to expect when you book a wedding or event
- Behind-the-scenes notes on how iPhone videography actually works day-of
- Gear, prep, and planning questions I get asked most often

I'll be adding to this as real stories come in — so if you don't see much here yet, that's because it's genuinely new. Thanks for being early.
