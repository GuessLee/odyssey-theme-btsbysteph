Couples booking a videographer for the first time usually have the same handful of questions. Here's the honest rundown.

## Before the day

We'll talk through your timeline together so I know when to show up and what matters most to you — getting ready, first look, ceremony, reception. There's no rigid shot list; the goal is to be close enough to catch the real moments without being in the way.

## On the day

I shoot on iPhone, deliberately. It's smaller, faster to move with, and lets me get shots a traditional rig can't — close, candid, unobtrusive. You'll notice me around, but I try hard not to be a production.

## After

Turnaround is quick by design. You'll get raw, authentic footage edited into something you'll actually want to rewatch — not just on your anniversary, but the week after, when you still can't quite believe it happened.
